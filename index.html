难度怎么判How difficulty is rated

每个 episode 按画面里占一半以上时间的主要活动来判，不按采集员的职业判。规则写成固定的评分标准，由视觉模型逐段打分并写出理由。标准可以换成客户自己的定义。Each episode is rated by the activity that fills more than half of its time, not by the operator's job title. The rules are a fixed rubric; a vision model scores every episode against it and writes its reason. The rubric can be swapped for the client's own definitions.

hard

需要训练或经验：精密工具、测量、诊断、多个前后依赖的步骤，做错会损坏、返工或有安全风险。Needs training or experience: precision tools, measurement, diagnosis, several dependent steps, where mistakes cause damage, rework or safety risk.

medium

多步骤的成人工作，需要协调和安排，但没有精密操作或诊断，比如清理工位、打包、看订单。Multi-step adult work that needs coordination but no precision or diagnosis, such as cleaning a station, packing or reading orders.

easy

谁都能做的单一重复动作，不用工具、不需要技术，比如浇水、搬东西、擦台面。One forgiving, repeated motion anyone could do, with no tools or skill, such as watering, carrying or wiping.

处理 pipelineProcessing pipeline

每段原始录像都走同一套流程。规则写在代码里，不靠人工每次判断；拿不准的片段放进人工复核，不直接交付。Every raw recording goes through the same steps. The rules live in code rather than in someone's judgment on the day; anything the pipeline is unsure about goes to human review instead of straight to delivery.

会被剔除的视频What gets filtered out

整段不要Reject

文件不全Missing files

头部视频、IMU、时间戳、标定文件任何一个缺失或为空。手腕文件缺了照常处理，在 metadata 里写明缺什么。Any head video, IMU, timestamp or calibration file missing or empty. A missing wrist file is still processed, with a flag in the metadata saying what is missing.

剪掉Cut

码流损坏、IMU 乱码Corrupt stream or IMU

连续超过 10 秒的坏帧或乱码剪掉。10 秒以内是设备常见现象，保留。Corrupt frames or garbled IMU longer than 10 s are cut out. Shorter glitches are a known device behaviour and are kept.

整段不要Reject

画面太暗或模糊Too dark or blurry

左目或右目超过一半时长太暗、太糊，整段不要。局部连续 10 秒以上，剪掉那一段。If either eye is too dark or blurry for more than half the recording, it is rejected. Shorter stretches of 10 s or more are cut.

整段不要 / 剪掉Reject / cut

没戴在头上Camera not worn

挂在胸前或放在桌上：陀螺仪连续 1 分钟低于 2°/s 判为没戴。全程没戴整段不要，部分时间没戴剪掉那段。Hung on the chest or left on a desk: gyro below 2°/s for a full minute means not worn. Not worn at all is rejected; not worn for part of the time is cut.

剪掉Cut

没有手No hands

手离开工作视野 10 秒以上剪掉。工作视野按客户去畸变后的视野计算，每台设备用自己的标定。Hands out of the working view for 10 s or more are cut. The working view follows the client's rectified field of view, computed from each device's own calibration.

剪掉Cut

玩手机、没在干活Phone use, idle time

手在画面里但没在干活：玩手机、闲聊、发呆、站着不动，持续一段时间就剪掉（默认 20 秒，可按项目调整）。Hands in view but not doing the task, such as using a phone, chatting or standing idle, are cut once it lasts long enough (20 s by default, adjustable per project).

整段不要Reject

工作区太小Work area too small

一半以上时间画面里主要是自己的身体，工作区不到画面 20%。For more than half the time the frame mostly shows the wearer's body and the work area covers less than 20% of it.

丢弃 / 切开Drop / split

太短或太长Too short or too long

短于 30 秒的片段丢弃。一个任务超过 30 分钟，在子任务的边界切成几个 episode。Pieces shorter than 30 s are dropped. A task longer than 30 minutes is split at sub-task boundaries.

标注Label

难度不够Not hard enough

只做简单重复动作、不用工具或不需要技术的片段标成 easy 或 medium，按客户要求决定交不交。Episodes that are one repeated motion with no tools or skill are labelled easy or medium, and delivered or held back as the client prefers.

阈值都可以按客户的规范调整。另外：时钟没设好的设备，时间按同一天其他录像推算并在 metadata 里写明；室内外、上午下午跨段的录像只在 metadata 里分段，不切开。Every threshold can be set to the client's spec. Recordings from a device whose clock was not set get estimated times, flagged in the metadata. Recordings that cross indoor/outdoor or morning/afternoon are segmented in the metadata, not cut.

Metadata 覆盖什么What the metadata covers

每个 episode 一份 metadata.json，字段名和取值都按客户的标签清单。场所、采集员、设备信息来自我们的登记表，任务和难度由 pipeline 看画面写出，交付前逐条检查有没有空值。Every episode ships with one metadata.json, with field names and values taken from the client's approved label lists. Site, operator and device fields come from our registration records; task and difficulty are written by the pipeline from the footage, and every record is checked for blanks before delivery.
